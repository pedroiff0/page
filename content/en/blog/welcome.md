> [!note] Summary
> A space for loose thoughts, tutorials and reflections on research, computing and everything else — separate from the more formal notes on the site.

The rest of the site is organized by subject — Research, Resources, Classes — each page with a well-defined purpose. This blog is the opposite: a looser space, for whatever doesn't fit anywhere else. Work-in-progress ideas, short tutorials, reflections on the research process (dead ends included, not just the results that make it to the poster), or anything else worth writing down.

No fixed publishing schedule — I only post here when I have something worth recording.

If you want to comment on a post, there's a comment box at the bottom of the page (via GitHub Discussions).
