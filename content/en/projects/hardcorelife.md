> [!note] In one line
> Personal modular platform, in **planning**, to bring together finances, investments, vehicles, tasks and family sharing in one place.

The core idea is **one system, many modules**, each covering a different area of personal life, all integrated instead of scattered across spreadsheets and standalone apps. Planned modules:

- **Financial control** and **investment control**
- **Vehicle control** and **task control**
- **Streaming subscriptions control**
- **Family sharing** — restricted access for whoever I choose to grant it

Access designed in two layers: web (open or restricted, depending on the module) and, eventually, a mobile app with restricted access.

**Status:** still in the design phase — modular architecture, authentication and data organization. Agile methodology (Kanban/SCRUM via GitHub) and good UI/UX and documentation practices are part of the plan from the start.
