# 🛰️ IX FEBIC 2024

> [!note] Summary
> Brazilian Fair of Scientific Initiation, in Pomerode (SC) — 3rd place in the Undergraduate category with the satellite trail removal project, still incomplete at the time.

## 🗓️ About the event

Page dedicated to the participation in the **IX Brazilian Fair of Scientific Initiation (FEBIC)**, in Pomerode (SC).

Maycon Jorge Deláqua da Silva and I represented IFF Bom Jesus do Itabapoana with the project **"Simulating the impact of satellites on astronomical observations"**, about how artificial satellites cause light pollution and harm astronomical observations. We placed **3rd in the Undergraduate category**, even with the project still incomplete and competing against more advanced, already patented applications — a result that also qualified us for MCTIA 2025, in Belém (PA).
