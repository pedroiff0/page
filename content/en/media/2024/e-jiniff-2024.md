# 🎮 e-JINIFF 2024

> [!note] Summary
> I organized the Bom Jesus do Itabapoana campus e-sports team at the institutional stage of the Federal Network Electronic Games — 1st place in Valorant, 3rd in Free Fire.

## 🗓️ About the event

Page dedicated to the institutional stage of **e-JINIFF 2024 (Federal Network Electronic Games)**, organized by the Cabo Frio Campus with the participation of several IFF units, in August 2024.

I organized the Bom Jesus do Itabapoana campus team, which competed in five e-sports categories (plus a chess tournament). We won **1st place in Valorant**, with Marcus Vinicius (Technical Course in Computing), and **3rd place in Free Fire**, with the team formed by Carlos Henrique Brasil, Breno dos Santos Souza, Heitor Gabriel Lamão Pontes and Kaíque Cezar Lima. We also took part in League of Legends, with Márcio Alves Teixeira Júnior (Computer Engineering) as coach. The plan now is to expand e-sports on campus with training projects and our own tournament during October's Computing Week.
