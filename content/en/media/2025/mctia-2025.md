# 🛰️ MCTIA 2025

> [!note] Summary
> Instituto Açaí Science and Technology Fair, in Belém (PA) — 1st place in Exact Sciences with an AI algorithm recovering 99.7% of the information lost to satellite trails, qualifying the team for Ciência Jovem 2026.

## 🗓️ About the event

Page dedicated to the participation in the **XVI Instituto Açaí Science and Technology Fair (MCTIA)**, in Belém (PA) — the largest science fair in the North of Brazil.

Alongside Maycon Jorge Deláqua da Silva and Arthur Miguelito Lopes, under the supervision of Prof. Ana Cecília Soja, we presented the project **"Simulating the impact of satellites on astronomical observations"** — an AI algorithm that removes satellite trails from astronomical data, recovering 99.7% of the information that would otherwise be lost. We placed **1st in the Higher Education Exact Sciences category**, which qualified us for the national Ciência Jovem event, in Recife (PE), in 2026.
