# 🔭 SAB 2025

> [!note] Summary
> Annual Meeting of the Brazilian Astronomical Society, in Caxambu (MG) — I presented "Women in the Skies" (oral) and the poster on anomaly detection in Gaia/GALAH DR4 data.

## 🗓️ About the event

Page dedicated to the participation in the **Annual Meeting of the Brazilian Astronomical Society (SAB 2025)**, in Caxambu (MG), from September 28 to October 2, 2025 — the largest astronomy research meeting in Latin America, with about 500 participants and more than 200 presentations.

Alongside Prof. Ana Cecília Soja, I brought two works: **"Women in the Skies"** (oral presentation, on the representation of women in the history of constellations) and **"Stellar properties and chemical features of the Gaia Catalogue of Nearby Stars observed by GALAH DR4"** (poster, an analysis of about 6,000 stars using Gaia data). As I put it at the time: "in a congress there's no competition, you just want to show that you're doing science... but in a collaborative way."
