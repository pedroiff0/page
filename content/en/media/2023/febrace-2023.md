# 🔬 FEBRACE 2023

> [!note] Summary
> IFF Bom Jesus do Itabapoana's first-ever participation in FEBRACE, Brazil's largest science and engineering fair — a dark matter project selected among 200 finalists out of more than 3,200 submissions.

## 🗓️ About the event

Page dedicated to the participation in the **21st Brazilian Science and Engineering Fair (FEBRACE)**, in São Paulo, on March 20, 2023 — the largest science and engineering fair in Brazil.

It was the first time IFF's Bom Jesus do Itabapoana campus took part in FEBRACE. Under the supervision of Prof. Ana Cecília Soja, I brought the project **"Analysis of dynamic simulations of merging galaxy clusters"**, about dark matter from galaxy cluster collision data. The project was initially rejected, advanced after a revision, and ended up selected among the **200 finalists** out of more than 3,200 submissions.
