# 🖥️ LNCC 2022

> [!note] Summary
> Technical visit to the National Laboratory for Scientific Computing, in Petrópolis (RJ), to see the Santos Dumont supercomputer — the largest in Latin America.

## 🗓️ About the event

Page dedicated to the technical visit to the **National Laboratory for Scientific Computing (LNCC)**, in Petrópolis (RJ), to see the **Santos Dumont** supercomputer — the largest in Latin America.

I went with colleagues from the Technical Course in Computing and Computer Engineering, including Halisson José Soares da Rocha, then in his 5th term. We toured the Santos Dumont facilities, which have more than 36,000 processing cores and rank among the world's top 500 supercomputers, as well as the LNCC's specialized library and its historical computing equipment collection — a good way to connect what we see in the classroom with scientific computing in practice.
