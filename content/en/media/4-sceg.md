## About the event

Page dedicated to the **4th Computing, Engineering and Management Week (SCEG)**, at IFF Bom Jesus do Itabapoana, in October 2024, part of the XII Knowledge Exhibition programming.

I was the event organizer, which prioritized student leadership: digital art (ibisPaint X) and cryptography minicourses, e-sports competitions (Valorant, Counter-Strike, FIFA), a hackathon to develop parking-monitoring software, and a discussion circle on women in computing. As I highlighted at the time, most of the activities were run by students rather than professors — to show, in practice, the benefits of peer-based learning.

Source: [IFF — Technology, innovation, e-sports and training mark the 4th SCEG programming](https://portal1.iff.edu.br/nossos-campi/bom-jesus-do-itabapoana/noticias/tecnologia-inovacao-e-sports-e-capacitacao-marcam-programacao-da-4-sceg) (Portuguese)
